Research publications from Juan Luis Varona

Welcome to Juan L. Varona web pages. Here you can download most of my research articles and related material, such as chapters of books and proceedings of conferences, my Ph. D. thesis,... (actually, some of the papers in the below list are, mainly, mathematical divulgation; especially, those written in Spanish). Unless otherwise stated, all of them are .pdf and/or .tex files, perhaps compressed. In most cases, links to the final published version are also provided.

Please, send me a message if you try to download a file without success.

Note that some papers can have an appearance and a format different to those used in the corresponding journal. Also, perhaps the final version in the journal may have some English and other minor changes.

External links to some pages in databases of papers and/or reviews: MathSciNet (AMS), zbMATH Open (Zentralblatt MATH), ORCID, ResearchGate, Google Scholar, Scopus, Publons, Dialnet, Documat, Portal de investigación de la Universidad de La Rioja.

Some of my coauthors have their own web pages: Fernando Albiac, Wolfgang Creyaufmüller, Antonio J. Durán, Armengol Gasull, T. Alastair Gillespie, José Manuel Gutiérrez, José María Grau, Andrew N. W.
